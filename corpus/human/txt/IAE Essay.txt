"Evaluate the economic impact of electric vehicle adoption."

Electric vehicles (EVs) are new challengers for fuel vehicles,
given their unexpected impacts on the business environment.
Stemming from the incentivizing policies, the increase of
electric cars is unstoppable but the popularity of traditional
cars are fading. Not only can EVs bring about benefits to the
environment, they can also contribute to the technology
spillovers and create more opportunities for different
businesses. Although the adoption of electric vehicles will
benefit the economy, many worried that too much investment
on EVs will disrupt the government’s major investments.
Therefore, this essay will analyze the economic influence of
electric vehicles in three aspects: the creation of business
opportunities, the potential to stimulate consumption, and the
increase in the government expenditure.
 
Firstly, the adoption of electric vehicles can create business
opportunities through promoting the formation of a circular
economy. According to Chirumalla et al. (2022), as more
people drive electric vehicles, the demand for EV batteries

would increase rapidly. A battery's service life is around 7 to
15 years and millions of EV batteries will end their life cycle
in the next several years. But since these batteries still retain
70% to 80% of their capacity, the second life of EV batteries
starts at their inclusion in circular business models such as the
recycling industry. Not only will the recycling industry
benefit from the recycling and reusing trend, the solar panel
industry can be more technologically advanced through the
integration of used EV batteries (Beaudet et al., 2020). Hence,
the adoption of electric cars can create potential business
opportunities for seemingly unrelated industries.
 
Secondly, the use of electric vehicles can stimulate
consumption. Appropriate government strategies can reduce
the costs for EV ownership. For instance, a good charging
schedule can help them save money and make sure the
charging infrastructure is adequate for daily use (Chung et al.,
2018). Patil et al. (2021) explained that many countries
provide financial incentives or other benefits to those who
generate energy for the grid, ultimately encouraging people to
buy and utilize electric vehicles. When the cost of owning
electric vehicles is lower, it will encourage more people to
buy electric vehicles instead of traditional cars. If people have
more money, their motivation to make purchasing decisions
will be increased. Thus, consumption will rise and it will
propel growth in the GDP.
 
However, the adoption of electric vehicles will burden
government expenditures. In order to encourage the public to
use electric vehicles instead of traditional cars, many
countries provide a variety of incentivizing policies. However,
it creates a heavy burden on the government. The Danish
government provided a 180% import tax rebate on electric

vehicles since they wanted to promote high penetration and
the Georgian government provided a $5000 tax credit to every
EV driver (Albrahim et al., 2019). Given rapid growth in the
adoption of electric vehicles, these financial subsidies and
policy support are becoming unsustainable. Governments had
to provide a lot of direct incentives to increase electric vehicle
sales, but affected other social investments or welfare
expenses (Sheldon, 2022). Under this situation, the adoption
of vehicles will create a heavy economic burden to the
government and reduce public expenditures on other issues. It
will harm society and stifle other potentially meaningful
investments.
 
To conclude, this essay has outlined three economic
influences about the adoption of electric vehicles. It not only
can create business opportunities and jobs for the society, but
also the cost of using them is cheaper than the traditional cars.
It means users can save money and have the potential to earn
money so they will increase consumption willingly. However,
the government providing subsidies to promote electric
vehicles will increase their expenditure and other investment.
Overall, the adoption of electric vehicles is a great substitute
to the traditional cars, but the government needs to consider
different measures to encourage public use.
