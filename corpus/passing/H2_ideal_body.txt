By applying this aim-setting method specifically, I will not feel burdened by arduous academic tasks. As to 'M'(Measureable) criteria, I will keep on revising exam for 6 hours a week, that is from 7 pm to 9 pm, 2 hours for each session. on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. I'm going to keep track of my study progress using Yeolpumta app. It will give me the details of how long I studied and what I learned. In order to test my understanding, I will solve a set of past papers. If i get less than 70% accuracy, the next week i will spend extra time working on these weak topics. For "A" (achievable), I will have a quiet place set up at home for these regular scheduled fix-up sessions. The function of blocking application in Yeolpumta app is useful to avoid being distracted by digital devices. In order to make sure I'm consistent, if I happen to have some sort of un-avoidable event, I'll push it off to the next night (so it'll be this coming evening) to stay on track for the week. As to 'R'(Relevant) criteria, by controlling my regular study plan, my internal locus of control and positive self-concept will be enhanced. It proves that academic achievement is subject to consistent personal efforts (Hanna et al., 2010). As to 'T'(Time-bound) criteria, I will firmly go on with such structured plan in 1 month. and check my progress in every week.
